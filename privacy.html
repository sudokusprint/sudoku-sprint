Privacy

Last updated October 2, 2026

Sudoku Sprint is a free puzzle game. This page explains what it stores about you and why.

Playing without an account

Your stats (best times, puzzles solved, race record) and your board style are saved only in your own browser. Nothing about you is sent to us.

If you create an account

We store:

  • Your email address, used only to send you sign-in codes. It is never shown to other players.
  • Your username, which is public so other players can see you, for example on leaderboards.
  • Your game results: for each finished game, the mode, difficulty, time, mistakes, and when you played. These are private to you unless a feature (like a leaderboard) says otherwise.
  • Progress you had as a guest, if you chose to move it into your account when you signed up.

Accounts and game results are stored by Supabase, which runs our database. The game itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may log basic request details such as IP addresses. Its fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which works the same way.

What we don't do

  • No ads and no advertising trackers.
  • No selling or sharing of your data.
  • No analytics or tracking cookies. Your browser keeps your sign-in session and settings in its local storage, and that's all.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete account. This permanently removes your email address, username, and all saved game results. Guest progress lives in your browser; clearing your browser's site data removes it.

Children

Sudoku Sprint isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date. Questions? Open an issue on the game's GitHub page.
